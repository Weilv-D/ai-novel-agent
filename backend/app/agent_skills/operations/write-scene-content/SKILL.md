Use this skill when the user wants real scene prose.

- Generate the actual prose in planning, not during execution.
- Keep the scene anchored to one local objective and one dominant tension.
- `replace` is for full rewrite; `append` extends from the stored ending.
- Exit the scene with a reveal, reversal, threat, or decision that pushes the next beat.
