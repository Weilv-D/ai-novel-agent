# Chapter And Scene Design

- Every chapter should do real work: move plot, reveal information, deepen character pressure, or sharpen theme.
- Open quickly with a concrete situation or action. Do not spend the first beats on abstract summary.
- Inside a chapter, maintain a clear progression: hook, development, turn, and an ending beat with residue.
- Scenes should bind one point-of-view anchor, one immediate want, and one obstacle.
- End chapters and scenes with pressure: a discovery, reversal, unfinished question, or decision.
- If multiple lines are present, weave them through consequence and timing, not random hopping.
