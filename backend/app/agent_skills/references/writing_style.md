# Writing Style

- Prefer an elevated, restrained Chinese prose style with clear imagery and controlled rhythm.
- Characters should speak according to status and relationship. Rank and formality matter.
- Internal reasoning should feel analytical and strategic, especially in conflict-heavy scenes.
- Use concrete sensory details, decisive actions, and aftermath instead of vague emotional summary.
- For xianxia or power-structure material, keep terms, names, and artifacts specific and internally consistent.
- Avoid modern slang, meta commentary, and empty filler.
